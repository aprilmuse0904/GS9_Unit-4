LANGUAGE FOCUS

Explaining purpose: to + V₀

Use to + base verb to explain why people build, visit or preserve a place, cook traditional food, or follow a custom.

🏰 Monuments

They built a memorial to commemorate war heroes.

People visit the temple to worship.

People built castles to defend their land.

🍟 Cuisine

Restaurants share recipes to promote English cuisine.

Families cook old dishes to keep traditions alive.

🏮 Family traditions

We celebrate death anniversaries to honour our ancestors.

We gather together to pay respect to older generations.

Extra practice · Complete the purpose

1. We visit the monument to _______ our heroes. (commemorate)

2. People built city walls to _______ the city. (defend)

3. We learn family stories to _______ traditions alive. (keep)

4. Restaurants share recipes to _______ local cuisine. (promote)

PART C · PRESENTATION SKILLS

How to open a presentation

Technique 1 · Show a picture

“Good morning, everyone! Look at this picture. What can you see? Today, I'd like to talk about how we remember the past.”

Technique 2 · Set a scene

“Imagine you are standing in front of an ancient castle. You can see its strong stone walls. Why was it built? Let's find out!”
